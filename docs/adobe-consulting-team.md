# Building a World-Class Adobe Consulting Firm: Roles & Team Sizing

> Companion to [`adobe-platform-snapshot.md`](./adobe-platform-snapshot.md). Written November 2026.
> Use it to plan hiring, to scope client engagements in the app, and to show clients who would staff their work.

## 1. How Adobe practices are usually organized

Top Adobe partners (Adobe Platinum/Gold Solution Partners) staff along **three axes**:

1. **Product practices.** Deep specialists per product family: Data & CDP, Analytics, Personalization, Journeys & Campaigns, Content (AEM), and Commerce.
2. **Cross-cutting disciplines.** Architecture, data engineering, QA, privacy, AI, and project delivery. These people work across every product practice.
3. **Business & strategy.** Strategy, change management, enablement, sales, and partner management.

Two things matter here. First, **one person can cover several roles early on**. Second, Adobe partner tiers depend partly on how many of your people hold **Adobe certifications**, so certification is a hiring and training requirement, not a nice-to-have.

## 3. How many people? Three growth stages

### Stage 1: Boutique (6–10 people)
Each person wears several hats. This team can deliver AEP + RT-CDP + CJA + Target + Web SDK well, and simpler AJO.

| Seat | Covers roles |
|---|---|
| You: Principal / Solution Architect | 1, 8, 4, 41 |
| AEP Data Architect / Data Engineer | 9, 15, 16 |
| Data Collection Engineer | 12, 13, 14, 35 |
| CJA + Analytics Consultant | 20, 21, 22 |
| Target / Personalization Consultant-Developer | 23, 24 |
| AJO Consultant / Developer | 17, 18, 19 |
| Engagement Manager / BA | 38, 40 |
| *(add when needed)* AEM Lead Developer | 10, 25, 26 |
| *(add when needed)* AI / Integration Engineer | 11, 32 |

**Gaps you would subcontract or partner for:** large AEM builds, deliverability at scale, Commerce, and Marketo.

### Stage 2: Full-service practice (25–40 people)
- **Leadership (3):** Practice Lead, Delivery Director, Partner Manager
- **Strategy (3):** CX Strategist, Change/Enablement Lead, AI Strategy Lead
- **Architecture (4):** 2 AEP Solution Architects, 1 Data Architect, 1 AEM Architect
- **Data Collection & Engineering (6):** 3 Web/Mobile SDK engineers, 1 Event Forwarding engineer, 2 AEP data engineers
- **Products (12):** 2 RT-CDP, 3 AJO (journey, channel, developer), 2 CJA, 1 Analytics, 1 Analyst, 2 Target, 1 Data Scientist
- **AEM (5):** 2 back-end developers, 2 front-end/EDS developers, 1 DevOps
- **AI (1–2):** Agent / MCP developer
- **QA, Governance & Ops (3):** QA, Privacy, Managed Services
- **Delivery & Business (4–5):** 2 Engagement Managers, 1 BA, 1 Pre-sales, 1 Sales

That adds up to about 40. Run it at around 30 with a few combined seats.

### Stage 3: World-class / Platinum-scale (60–150+ people)
Duplicate each Stage 2 pod by industry vertical (retail, financial services, healthcare, travel, media). Then add:
- Dedicated AEM, Commerce, Marketo/B2B, and Workfront/GenStudio practices of 8–20 people each
- A managed-services desk for 24/7 platform operations
- An offshore or nearshore delivery center for cost leverage
- An R&D / accelerator team that builds reusable assets (schema templates, Tags libraries, journey templates, Coworker skills, the client-facing app)

A good **staffing ratio** to aim for is about **1 architect per 5–7 delivery engineers and consultants**, plus **1 engagement manager per 2–3 active programs**.

## 5. Certifications to require (Adobe Certified Professional / Expert / Master)
- **AEP / RT-CDP:** Developer Expert, Business Practitioner, Technical Foundations
- **AJO:** Developer Expert, Business Practitioner
- **CJA:** Business Practitioner, Developer Expert
- **Adobe Analytics:** Business Practitioner, Developer, Architect Master
- **Target:** Business Practitioner, Developer
- **AEM:** Sites Developer, Back-End Developer, Front-End Developer, DevOps Engineer, Architect Master
- **Workfront, Marketo, Commerce:** their respective tracks

Adobe renames and adds exams often, so check the current list on Adobe's certification site before you set hiring requirements.

## 6. Recommended first hires, given your strengths
You are strongest in the data and engineering layer (AEP, Data Collection, Target, CJA, AA). Your gaps are AJO and AEM, so hire in this order:
1. **AJO Journey Architect / Developer.** AJO is the fastest-growing AEP app and pairs naturally with your CDP and data work.
2. **AEM Architect / Lead Developer**, or a white-label partnership with an AEM boutique to start.
3. **Engagement Manager / BA**, so you can stay billable as the architect.
4. **AI / Integration Engineer**, to build Coworker skills, MCP integrations, and your client-facing app.
5. A **second Data Collection and Data Engineer**, so you can take on two programs at once.
